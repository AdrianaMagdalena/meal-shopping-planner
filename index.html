Meal & Shopping Planner

How to use the planner

Recipes

  • Search for the recipes under "Recipes" menu.
  • Open the filter panel to filter by meal and diet categories.
  • Add the recipe to your weekly plan by clicking on the button, then selecting days of the week.
  • Click on the star button to add to favorites
  • Open the recipe preview by clicking on a specific recipe.

Recipe preview

  • See the ingredients and instructions on how to prepare the recipe.
  • Adjust recipe's servings amount to your liking and add to weekly plan by clicking on the button, then selecting days of the week.
  • Click on the star button to add to favorites

Planner

  • Preview your plan for the week under "Planner" menu.
  • Adjust servings, remove specific entires or all entries entirely.
  • Generate your weekly recipe and shopping list by clicking on the button.

Weekly lists

  • Access your weekly recipe and shopping lists easily under "Week's list" menu.
  • While shopping, check bought items and remove the alrteady bought items from the list.
  • If you wish to clear the lists, remove the week's data entirely bu clicking on the button.

Favorites

  • Access your favorie recipes easily, alphabetically sorted.
  • Add the recipes to your weekly plan here or remove from favorites if needed.
